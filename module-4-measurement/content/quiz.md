# Electronics Quiz

## Question 1
When using a digital multimeter to measure the voltage ($V$) across a resistor in an active Arduino circuit, how should the probes be connected?

- [x] In parallel with the resistor
- [ ] In series with the resistor
- [ ] Between the resistor and the battery's positive terminal only
- [ ] In place of the resistor

**Hint:** Think about whether you need to 'bridge' across the component or become a part of the single-path loop.

## Question 2
To measure the electrical current ($I$) flowing through a circuit, what must you do with the circuit wiring and the multimeter?

- [x] Break the circuit and place the meter in series so current flows through it
- [ ] Keep the circuit closed and touch the probes to the battery terminals
- [ ] Connect the multimeter in parallel with the LED or load
- [ ] Touch both probes to the same copper trace on the breadboard

**Hint:** Electrical current is like water flow; to measure it, the 'flow' must pass through your measuring device.

## Question 3
What is the most important safety precaution to take before measuring the resistance ($R$) of a specific component?

- [x] Ensure the power to the circuit is completely turned off
- [ ] Switch the multimeter to the $10A$ high current setting
- [ ] Make sure the component is still connected to the $5V$ pin of the Arduino
- [ ] Heat the component with a soldering iron to improve conductivity

**Hint:** The multimeter provides its own energy source to test how much a component 'fights' the flow of electricity.

## Question 4
A digital multimeter set to 'Continuity' mode emits a 'beep' sound when the probes touch two different points. What does this indicate?

- [x] There is a continuous, low-resistance path between the two points
- [ ] The circuit is currently powered by a $9V$ battery
- [ ] The resistance between the points is infinitely high
- [ ] The multimeter has detected a short circuit that will blow a fuse

**Hint:** This mode is commonly used to check if a wire is broken or if a solder joint is making a good connection.

## Question 5
In a standard circuit diagram, what component is represented by a symbol consisting of a triangle pointing toward a line with two arrows pointing away from it?

- [x] A Light-Emitting Diode (LED)
- [ ] A standard Resistor
- [ ] A DC Power Supply
- [ ] A Push-button Switch

**Hint:** Look for the part of the symbol that suggests energy or light is being radiated outward from the component.

## Question 6

- [ ] It can measure the exact weight and physical size of an Arduino board
- [ ] It provides a higher current than a standard battery can supply

**Hint:** Think of this tool as providing a 'visual history' or a graph of the electricity's behavior.

## Question 7

## Question 8
On an oscilloscope screen, what property of the electrical signal is plotted on the horizontal ($X$) axis?

- [x] Time ($t$)
- [ ] Voltage ($V$)
- [ ] Current ($I$)
- [ ] Resistance ($R$)

**Hint:** This axis moves from left to right as the signal progresses through its cycle.

## Question 9
