# Electronics Flashcards

## Card 1

**Q:** What does the acronym "DMM" stand for in electronics?

**A:** Digital Multimeter

---

## Card 2

**Q:** To measure voltage across a component, the multimeter probes must be connected in _____ with that component.

**A:** parallel

---

## Card 3

**Q:** To measure the current flowing through a circuit, the multimeter must be connected in _____ with the circuit.

**A:** series

---

## Card 4

**Q:** What is the standard unit of measurement for electrical resistance?

**A:** Ohm ($\Omega$)

---

## Card 5

**Q:** Which multimeter setting is used to check for a continuous electrical path between two points?

**A:** Continuity

---

## Card 6

**Q:** An oscilloscope displays the relationship between voltage and _____.

**A:** time

---

## Card 7

**Q:** On an oscilloscope screen, which axis typically represents the voltage level?

**A:** The vertical axis (Y-axis)

---

## Card 8

**Q:** On an oscilloscope screen, which axis represents the passage of time?

**A:** The horizontal axis (X-axis)

---

## Card 9

**Q:** What is the standard unit of measurement for electrical potential difference?

**A:** Volt ($V$)

---

## Card 10

**Q:** What is the standard unit of measurement for the flow of electric charge?

**A:** Ampere ($A$)

---

## Card 11

**Q:** Before measuring resistance with a multimeter, the circuit's power must be turned _____.

**A:** off

---

## Card 12

**Q:** A "beep" sound during a continuity test indicates a _____ resistance path.

**A:** low

---

## Card 13

**Q:** What do schematic symbols represent in a circuit diagram?

**A:** Electronic components

---

## Card 14

**Q:** Which tool is primarily used for making static measurements of constant voltage or resistance?

**A:** Digital Multimeter

---

## Card 15

**Q:** Which tool allows a user to observe how a signal's voltage changes over a period of time?

**A:** Oscilloscope

---

## Card 16

**Q:** Why must the circuit be broken to measure current with a standard multimeter?

**A:** To force the current to flow through the meter for measurement.

---

## Card 17

**Q:** When measuring an unknown voltage, it is safest to start the multimeter on the _____ range setting.

**A:** highest

---

## Card 18

**Q:** In a schematic, a series of alternating long and short parallel lines usually represents a _____.

**A:** battery

---

## Card 19

**Q:** A zigzag line in an electronics schematic represents a _____.

**A:** resistor

---

## Card 20

**Q:** If a multimeter shows "OL" while measuring resistance, it indicates the resistance is _____.

**A:** infinite (or an open circuit)

---

## Card 21

**Q:** What is the primary advantage of using an oscilloscope instead of a multimeter?

**A:** It provides a visual waveform of how voltage varies over time.

---

## Card 22

**Q:** The black probe of a multimeter is almost always connected to the terminal labeled _____.

**A:** COM (Common)

---

## Card 23

**Q:** Concept: Schematic Diagram

**A:** Definition: A graphical representation of a circuit using standardized symbols to show electrical connections.

---

## Card 24

**Q:** On an oscilloscope, the "Vertical" scale controls the _____ per division on the grid.

**A:** Volts

---

## Card 25

**Q:** On an oscilloscope, the "Horizontal" scale controls the _____ per division on the grid.

**A:** time

---

## Card 26

**Q:** When measuring current, the multimeter acts like a _____ in the circuit.

**A:** short circuit (or wire)

---

## Card 27

**Q:** Why is it hazardous to measure current by placing multimeter probes in parallel with a power source?

**A:** It can cause a short circuit and blow the meter's internal fuse.

---

## Card 28

**Q:** In a schematic, a straight line represents a _____ connecting different components.

**A:** conductor (or wire)

---

## Card 29

**Q:** The unit abbreviation for Ohms is the Greek letter _____.

**A:** Omega ($\Omega$)

---

## Card 30

**Q:** Term: Continuity

**A:** Definition: An unbroken path that allows electric current to flow through a circuit.

---

## Card 31

**Q:** What is the result of reversing the probe leads when measuring $DC$ voltage?

**A:** The multimeter displays a negative voltage value.

---

## Card 32

**Q:** Which multimeter function is most effective for finding a break in a long wire?

**A:** Continuity

---

## Card 33

**Q:** In a schematic, the symbol "GND" represents the _____ point of the circuit.

**A:** ground (or common reference)

---

## Card 34

**Q:** What does a schematic symbol consisting of two parallel lines of equal length represent?

**A:** A capacitor

---

## Card 35

**Q:** The visual graph produced by an oscilloscope is known as a _____.

**A:** waveform

---

## Card 36

**Q:** A multimeter set to the "A" symbol is measuring _____.

**A:** current (Amperes)

---

## Card 37

**Q:** A multimeter set to the "V" symbol is measuring _____.

**A:** voltage (Volts)

---

## Card 38

**Q:** When measuring resistance, the component being tested should be _____ from the rest of the circuit.

**A:** isolated (or removed)

---

## Card 39

**Q:** The ability of a multimeter to select the most appropriate measurement scale automatically is called _____.

**A:** autoranging

---

## Card 40

**Q:** Which tool would a beginner use to determine if a signal is a square wave or a sine wave?

**A:** Oscilloscope

---

## Card 41

**Q:** A schematic symbol with a triangle pointing toward a line represents a _____.

**A:** diode

---

## Card 42

**Q:** In a schematic, a dot at the intersection of two lines indicates an _____.

**A:** electrical connection (or junction)

---

## Card 43

**Q:** What happens to the waveform's appearance on an oscilloscope if the "Volts per Division" is increased?

**A:** The waveform appears smaller vertically.

---

## Card 44

**Q:** What happens to the waveform's appearance on an oscilloscope if the "Time per Division" is decreased?

**A:** The waveform appears more spread out horizontally.

---

## Card 45

**Q:** Which unit of frequency, representing cycles per second, is often measured on an oscilloscope?

**A:** Hertz ($Hz$)

---

## Card 46

**Q:** A schematic symbol with two circles and a movable line indicates a _____.

**A:** switch

---

## Card 47

**Q:** When measuring high current, the red probe is typically moved to a specific _____ port on the multimeter.

**A:** $10A$ (or High Amperage)

---

## Card 48

**Q:** What is the primary purpose of schematic diagrams for someone building an Arduino project?

**A:** To provide a clear map of how components must be connected.

---

## Card 49

**Q:** The standard color for the positive probe on a multimeter is _____.

**A:** red

---

## Card 50

**Q:** The standard color for the common or negative probe on a multimeter is _____.

**A:** black

---

## Card 51

**Q:** If a multimeter shows a very low resistance value (near $0\Omega$) during a continuity test, is the circuit path open or closed?

**A:** Closed

---

## Card 52

**Q:** Which electronic tool is best suited for identifying fast-acting noise or spikes in a power supply?

**A:** Oscilloscope

---

## Card 53

**Q:** To measure the voltage of a standard AA battery, the multimeter should be set to _____ voltage.

**A:** $DC$ (Direct Current)

---

## Card 54

**Q:** A schematic symbol of a circle with a "V" inside represents a(n) _____.

**A:** voltmeter

---

## Card 55

**Q:** A schematic symbol of a circle with an "A" inside represents a(n) _____.

**A:** ammeter
