# Introduction to Electronics — Module 1: Safety & Fundamentals
Target audience: complete beginners (~16 years old) preparing to build with Arduino.
Learning objectives:
- Electrical safety rules: shock hazards, fire risks, short-circuit dangers, and how to avoid them.
- Safe lab practices and correct handling of tools (soldering irons, cutters, multimeters).
- How to read a component datasheet: pinouts, absolute maximum ratings, voltage/current limits.
