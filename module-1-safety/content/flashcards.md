# Electronics Flashcards

## Card 1

**Q:** Which specific electrical hazard involves current flowing through a human body?

**A:** Shock hazard.

---

## Card 2

**Q:** What is the primary risk associated with components that overheat due to excessive current?

**A:** Fire hazard.

---

## Card 3

**Q:** A(n) _____ occurs when a low-resistance path is accidentally created between two points in a circuit, leading to excessive current.

**A:** short-circuit

---

## Card 4

**Q:** What is the most common physical danger of a short-circuit to electronic components?

**A:** Permanent damage due to overheating.

---

## Card 5

**Q:** How can a builder physically prevent short-circuits during the wiring process?

**A:** By ensuring no exposed wires or conductive terminals touch each other.

---

## Card 6

**Q:** Which lab tool reaches high temperatures and requires a dedicated stand for safety?

**A:** Soldering iron.

---

## Card 7

**Q:** Why is a soldering iron stand essential for safe lab practice?

**A:** It prevents the hot tip from making contact with flammable surfaces or skin.

---

## Card 8

**Q:** What is the standard safety practice regarding a soldering iron's power after finishing a task?

**A:** Unplug it or turn off the power station.

---

## Card 9

**Q:** To prevent flying debris from hitting your eyes, in which direction should you point wire cutters when clipping leads?

**A:** Down toward the work surface.

---

## Card 10

**Q:** Why is it dangerous to clip component leads without shielding the cut or pointing it downward?

**A:** Small metal fragments can fly off at high speeds and cause eye injury.

---

## Card 11

**Q:** Which diagnostic tool is used to measure $Voltage$, $Current$, and $Resistance$ in a circuit?

**A:** Multimeter.

---

## Card 12

**Q:** What is a key safety precaution when using multimeter probes on a live circuit?

**A:** Ensure the probe tips do not bridge two points and cause a short-circuit.

---

## Card 13

**Q:** The _____ section of a datasheet provides a diagram mapping each physical pin to its specific function.

**A:** pinout

---

## Card 14

**Q:** What does the 'Absolute Maximum Ratings' section of a component datasheet define?

**A:** The limits beyond which a component may suffer permanent failure.

---

## Card 15

**Q:** Why must a builder verify 'Voltage Limits' in a datasheet before connecting a power source like an Arduino?

**A:** To ensure the supplied voltage ($V$) does not exceed the component's safe operating range.

---

## Card 16

**Q:** Which datasheet specification indicates the maximum flow of charge ($I$) a component can safely handle?

**A:** Current limit.

---

## Card 17

**Q:** What is the primary purpose of reading a component's datasheet before building a circuit?

**A:** To understand its pinout and ensure you stay within its electrical limits.

---

## Card 18

**Q:** Term: Short-circuit

**A:** Definition: An unintended, low-resistance connection that causes dangerously high current flow.

---

## Card 19

**Q:** In safe lab practices, what is the best way to avoid accidental fire from an unattended soldering iron?

**A:** Always power it down or unplug it when leaving the workspace.

---

## Card 20

**Q:** When considering the safety of a circuit, what role does a multimeter play before power is even applied?

**A:** It can be used to check for unintended short-circuits between power and ground.

---

## Card 21

**Q:** How does knowing a component's pinout prevent circuit failure?

**A:** It ensures that power and signals are applied to the correct leads of the component.

---

## Card 22

**Q:** If a datasheet lists an 'Absolute Maximum' $Voltage$ of $5\text{V}$, what is the risk of using a $9\text{V}$ battery?

**A:** The component will likely suffer permanent damage or failure.

---

## Card 23

**Q:** Safe lab practices suggest that wire cutters should always be kept _____ when not in use.

**A:** closed and in a designated storage area

---

## Card 24

**Q:** What is the safest way to dispose of sharp metal clippings produced by wire cutters?

**A:** Sweep them into a bin immediately to prevent them from causing shorts or injuries.

---
