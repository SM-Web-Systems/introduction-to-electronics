# Electronics Flashcards

## Card 1

**Q:** What is the primary function of a resistor in an electronic circuit?

**A:** To limit the flow of electrical current.

---

## Card 2

**Q:** The SI unit used to measure resistance is the _____.

**A:** Ohm ($\Omega$)

---

## Card 3

**Q:** What does the 'tolerance' rating of a resistor indicate?

**A:** The allowable percentage of deviation from the resistor's nominal value.

---

## Card 4

**Q:** In a standard four-band resistor, what information is provided by the third band?

**A:** The multiplier.

---

## Card 5

**Q:** Which band on a four-band resistor identifies the tolerance?

**A:** The fourth band.

---

## Card 6

**Q:** What resistance tolerance is represented by a gold-colored band?

**A:** $\pm 5\%$

---

## Card 7

**Q:** What resistance tolerance is represented by a silver-colored band?

**A:** $\pm 10\%$

---

## Card 8

**Q:** Why is a resistor typically placed in series with an LED?

**A:** To limit the current and prevent the LED from burning out.

---

## Card 9

**Q:** Concept: Fixed Resistor

**A:** Definition: A component with a single, non-adjustable resistance value.

---

## Card 10

**Q:** Concept: Potentiometer

**A:** Definition: A type of variable resistor that allows manual adjustment of resistance.

---

## Card 11

**Q:** What is the primary function of a capacitor?

**A:** To store electrical energy in an electric field.

---

## Card 12

**Q:** The unit of measurement for capacitance is the _____.

**A:** Farad ($F$)

---

## Card 13

**Q:** What does it mean for a capacitor to be 'polarized'?

**A:** It has a specific positive and negative terminal and must be oriented correctly.

---

## Card 14

**Q:** Which common type of capacitor is typically polarized?

**A:** Electrolytic capacitor.

---

## Card 15

**Q:** Which common type of capacitor is typically non-polarized?

**A:** Ceramic capacitor.

---

## Card 16

**Q:** What are the two most critical ratings found on a capacitor's casing?

**A:** Capacitance value and maximum voltage rating.

---

## Card 17

**Q:** What is a potential consequence of connecting a polarized capacitor backward in a circuit?

**A:** The component may fail or explode.

---

## Card 18

**Q:** What is the primary characteristic of a diode regarding current flow?

**A:** It allows current to flow in only one direction.

---

## Card 19

**Q:** What does the acronym LED stand for?

**A:** Light Emitting Diode.

---

## Card 20

**Q:** In diode terminology, what is the name of the positive terminal?

**A:** Anode.

---

## Card 21

**Q:** In diode terminology, what is the name of the negative terminal?

**A:** Cathode.

---

## Card 22

**Q:** How can you physically identify the cathode lead on a standard through-hole LED?

**A:** It is the shorter lead and is often marked by a flat edge on the plastic casing.

---

## Card 23

**Q:** What is the term for the state where a diode allows current to flow?

**A:** Forward bias.

---

## Card 24

**Q:** What is the term for the state where a diode blocks current from flowing?

**A:** Reverse bias.

---

## Card 25

**Q:** How is the cathode typically marked on the body of a standard signal diode?

**A:** With a colored stripe or band.

---

## Card 26

**Q:** What are the two main configurations of Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT)?

**A:** NPN and PNP.

---

## Card 27

**Q:** In basic electronics, what is the primary role of a transistor when used with an Arduino?

**A:** To act as an electronic switch.

---

## Card 28

**Q:** What are the three terminals of a Bipolar Junction Transistor?

**A:** Base, Collector, and Emitter.

---

## Card 29

**Q:** Which transistor terminal acts as the 'gatekeeper' or controller for current flow?

**A:** The Base.

---

## Card 30

**Q:** In an NPN transistor switch circuit, applying a 'High' signal to the _____ allows current to flow from Collector to Emitter.

**A:** Base

---

## Card 31

**Q:** How does the switching logic of a PNP transistor differ from an NPN transistor?

**A:** A PNP transistor typically turns on when the base is connected to a lower voltage.

---

## Card 32

**Q:** What is an inductor?

---

## Card 33

## Card 34

## Card 35
